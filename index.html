About Us

Open Technology Hub is a technology investment and ecosystem enabler focused on future high-potential open source projects. We believe that the next generation of technology forces that will change the industrial landscape often originates from "edge innovations" within the open source community.

With our core mission of "Discover, Incubate, Connect", we are committed to identifying early-stage open source projects with disruptive impact potential. Through equity participation via technical contributions, we deeply engage with project teams to build sustainable development ecosystems and drive their transition from code communities to real industries.

Our Vision

To ensure every world-changing open source project finds its path to industry

Our Core Model

Unlike traditional venture capital that primarily provides funding, and different from typical open source foundations that only focus on community governance, Open Technology Hub adopts a unique "Technology + Industry" dual-drive model:

💡

Tech Equity Partnership

We invest through technical contributions, architecture consulting, and ecosystem operations as intangible assets in exchange for project equity or long-term revenue sharing, forming partnerships with aligned interests and shared risks.

🎯

Transparent Industrial Implementation

For each partnered open source project, we co-develop public, measurable, phased industrial implementation roadmaps based on technical characteristics and team preferences. The entire process maintains appropriate transparency to ensure the open spirit is not compromised by commercial cooperation.

🌐

Cross-Industry Scenario Development

We have accumulated replicable open source implementation methodologies across multiple high-barrier industries, including AI+Healthcare, AI+Legal, AI+Training, industrial intelligence, open source hardware, and green computing.
