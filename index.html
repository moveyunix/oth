Open Technology Hub

Who We Are

Open Technology Hub is a technology investment & ecosystem empowerment institution focused on future high-potential open source projects. We believe disruptive innovations are born from 'edge innovation' within open source communities, and we dedicate to discovering, incubating and bridging those projects from code to real industry impact.

Our Mission

To enable every game-changing open source project to find its path to industry. We don't control communities — we provide transparent mechanisms, technical expertise and deep industrial resources to cross the gap between 'technically feasible' and 'industry-ready'.

Our Core Model

Technology equity + industry ecosystems — a unique symbiosis model beyond traditional VC and foundations.

Technology Equity

We invest via technical contributions, architecture consulting and ecosystem building in exchange for project equity or long-term revenue sharing. Full alignment of interests and risk sharing.

Transparent Industrialization

We co-create open, measurable, phased industry adoption roadmaps — compliance, lighthouse customers, packaged products — while keeping open source communities informed and respected.

Cross-Industry Deepening

Proven open source industrialization playbooks in high-barrier sectors. We focus on AI+Healthcare, AI+Legal, AI+Training, Industrial Intelligence, Green Computing and more.

Industry Deep Dive

Driving open source transformation in mission-critical industries.

AI+Healthcare

Medical imaging, clinical NLP, structured EMR, privacy-preserving computing — enabling open source models for compliant healthcare AI.

AI+Legal

Open-source legal text analytics, case matching, contract review — reducing costs and barriers to legal services.

AI+Training

Intelligent training content generation, virtual teachers, learning path recommendation systems — serving corporate L&D and online education.

Industrial AI & Open Hardware

Exploring smart manufacturing, edge computing, green computing — frontier sectors to empower industrial evolution.
